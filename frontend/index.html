Our Vision & Goals

Our Vision

To empower the maritime industry to confidently navigate the decarbonization era.

Our Goals

The EU's introduction of EU ETS (2024 for maritime) and FuelEU Maritime (2025 onwards) creates new challenges for shipowners and cargo owners, necessitating carbon allowance purchases and promoting low-carbon fuel use. Recognizing the critical need for trading platforms in this evolving landscape, our goal is to provide an essential solution for compliance, cost optimization, and the transition to sustainable shipping.
